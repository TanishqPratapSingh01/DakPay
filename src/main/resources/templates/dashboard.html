DakPay

Offline Mesh Payment Prototype

Spring Boot backend running
Offline-first payment infrastructure

Move a payment through the mesh — even without internet.

DakPay demonstrates deferred-settlement payments where an encrypted transaction travels across nearby virtual devices until an internet-connected bridge reaches the backend. The prototype focuses on encryption, mesh forwarding, replay protection, idempotency, and atomic settlement.
